# LucidNav – Lucid Nightmare Maze Navigator

![LucidNav Maze Map](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cristian-encalada/LucidNav/main/assets/img/overview-poi-icons.png) Real-time mapping and navigation addon for the **Endless Halls**, the maze required to obtain the **Lucid Nightmare** secret mount. No more drawing the maze on paper.

🌐 **Available in: English, Español, Deutsch, Português (BR), 简体中文** — LucidNav follows your WoW client language automatically, no settings needed.

**Only active inside the Endless Halls.**

***

## Quick Start

*   **1.** Enter the **Endless Halls** and open the addon: `/lnn`, `/ln`, or `/lucid`
*   **2.** Walk around — the map builds itself (Unexplored territory)
*   **3.** **Left-click on edges** (N/S/E/W) to mark walls
*   **4.** Found a rune/orb? Click its matching color marker
*   **5.** Pick a **Navigation Target** for directions with step counts
*   **6.** Mistake? **Right-click** a room to fix it, or use **Undo** / **Restore** a checkpoint

If you get teleported, click **"I got ported!"** immediately.

***

## Tips for Solving the Maze

*   Don't extinguish runes early — they're your navigation landmarks
*   Navigate to unexplored rooms first
*   **Save a checkpoint** at every milestone
*   Wrong wall link? **Right-click → Unlink / Detach / Delete** instead of resetting the whole map

### When does the maze reset?

The Endless Halls layout is **not** tied to the daily/weekly instance lockout — it resets once per day at **realm midnight (00:00 server time)**.

*   After the reset, the old map is invalid — click **New Map** and start fresh
*   Try to finish your run before the reset — server time, not your local/player time, is what counts

***

## Localization

LucidNav automatically displays in your WoW client's language — **English, Español** (`esES`/`esMX`), **Português** (`ptBR`), **Deutsch** (`deDE`), and **简体中文** (`zhCN`) are supported, with English as the fallback for any other locale. Translations are a first draft (not yet reviewed by native speakers) — corrections are welcome via GitHub issues or pull requests.
